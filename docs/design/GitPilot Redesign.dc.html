Quiet instrument panel.

GitPilot is opened dozens of times a day to answer one question: what needs my attention, and what is the one action? The direction is IDE-native and flat-utilitarian on the Nocturne system: a dense, keyboard-driven list, a sidebar for scope, no decoration that is not data. Color is reserved for state and identity, so a glance down the column is a reading, not a scan.
